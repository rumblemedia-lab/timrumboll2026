About Tim

Sheffield-based, remote-ready

Tim has been voicing professional work since 2008, and full-time since 2012 — mostly commercials, promos and explainer videos, alongside corporate narration, e-learning and documentary work.

He records from a treated home studio in Sheffield, with Source Connect Now available for live-directed sessions, or a fast self-produced turnaround for briefs that don't need a director on the line.

Natural Yorkshire tones, or neutral and broad on request — whichever the brief calls for.
